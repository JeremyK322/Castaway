Castaway

You are about to wake on an uncharted island. The story is authored by your chosen model — every playthrough is different.

Requires an API key. Set it in ⚙.

Instructions

1 / 5

Move

Tap any adjacent tile to walk to it. The map always shows your current tile in the centre, surrounded by the eight tiles around you.

Colours tell you what you're standing on:

  • Sand — the beach
  • Green — jungle
  • Olive — marsh
  • Brown — hills
  • Grey — cliff
  • Black — cave
  • Dark — ruins
  • Light blue — shallow water. You can wade, but the tide has teeth.
  • Dark blue — open sea. You cannot enter without a boat.

Some tiles are fogged. To see them, stand next to them.

Choices

Each place you visit may offer you choices. Tap one to act. Most choices take time.

Some choices are one-time only. If you salvage the wreck, the wreck is gone. If you take the journal page, it is not there when you return.

Some choices expire. A message will say "The ship has been lost to the sea." Tap it once to dismiss it.

Some places have nothing to offer. You will walk through them, see the land, and move on. That is the island. It has many quiet places.

Choices are remembered. When you return to a place, the same choices wait — unless time, weather, or the story has changed.

Time

One day is 24 hours. Every action costs hours. Sleeping, working, walking, waiting — all of it takes time.

When you choose a slow action, a keypad appears. Type the hours you'll spend, then tap Go.

Time is the only resource you cannot gather back. The sun sets whether you are ready or not.

Night is dangerous. Without fire, without shelter, a night in the open can take your health. Build a camp early.

Weather changes each day. Tap the icon in the top right to see what today brings — and what tomorrow will.

Your tools

The four buttons at the bottom are your hands:

  • Gather — pick up what this place offers. Wood on the beach, berries in the jungle, fresh water in the marsh. You choose how many hours to spend.
  • Craft — make tools, cook food, build camp. You can only build on the tile you are standing on.
  • Inventory — see everything you carry. Tap any food or water to eat or drink it.
  • Rest — sleep. Rest heals you and steadies your mind. A camp with a fire, a lean-to, a hut — each one makes rest better.

Your camp grows where you build it. Return to it to add more. A place with fire and shelter will keep you alive through storms.

Survival

Keep four things above zero: health, food, water, morale. If any one falls to nothing, you fall with it.

You eat and drink automatically when your food or water drops below a third. The island provides — but only if you carry it. A full pack cannot save an empty one.

Raw fish and raw meat can make you sick. Cook them at a campfire when you can. Berries and fresh water are safe, but give less.

You can also use items from your Inventory at any time — tap a berry to eat it early, tap a bandage to stop a wound from bleeding.

Water runs out faster than food. Build a well at camp. Collect rainwater in storms. Do not travel without a full canteen.

You are not alone on this island. There is a secret here — older than any ship. Follow what you find. Read what is written. The island has been waiting.

Good luck. The Perseverance was not the first ship to break on these shores.
